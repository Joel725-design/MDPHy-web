Practical MDPhy Tech develops practical digital tools, educational resources, and intelligent technology solutions for medical physics, radiation quality assurance, dosimetry, and scientific research.

Explore Our Projects Discover Our Services

Bridging medical physics and digital innovation.

Practical MDPhy Tech is a technology initiative focused on creating practical, research-informed tools for the medical physics community. We combine expertise in radiation physics, software development, and data science to build solutions that support quality assurance, dosimetry, and clinical decision-making.

Our work spans educational platforms, QA analytics, AI prototypes, and scientific software — always with a focus on clarity, reproducibility, and real-world utility.
